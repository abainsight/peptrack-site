PepTrack Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 30, 2026

Introduction

PepTrack ("we," "our," or "the app") is a GLP-1 medication companion app designed to help you track your injections, side effects, nutrition, and weight loss journey. Your privacy is important to us.

Information We Collect

Health & Wellness Data: PepTrack stores all your health tracking data — including injection logs, side effect reports, weight measurements, nutrition logs, progress photos, and medication information — locally on your device only. This data never leaves your phone and is not transmitted to any server.

No Account Required: PepTrack does not require you to create an account, sign in, or provide any personal information such as your name, email address, or phone number.

Purchase Information: If you subscribe to PepTrack Premium, your payment is processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. We do not collect, store, or have access to your payment details, credit card numbers, or billing information. We receive only a confirmation that a valid subscription exists.

Information We Do NOT Collect

  • We do not collect your name, email, or contact information
  • We do not collect your location data
  • We do not track your activity across other apps or websites
  • We do not use advertising identifiers or serve ads
  • We do not sell, share, or transfer any data to third parties
  • We do not use analytics or tracking SDKs

Data Storage & Security

All data is stored locally on your device using secure on-device storage. If you delete the app, all your data is permanently deleted. We recommend using your device's built-in backup features (iCloud Backup) if you wish to preserve your data.

Third-Party Services

Apple App Store: Subscription purchases are processed by Apple. Apple's privacy policy governs how they handle your payment information.

RevenueCat: We use RevenueCat to manage subscription status. RevenueCat receives only an anonymous app user ID and subscription status — no health data, personal information, or tracking data is shared. See RevenueCat's Privacy Policy.

Children's Privacy

PepTrack is not intended for use by children under the age of 17. We do not knowingly collect any information from children.

Your Rights

Since all data is stored locally on your device, you have complete control over your data at all times. You can delete all app data by deleting the app from your device.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected in the "Last updated" date above. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
